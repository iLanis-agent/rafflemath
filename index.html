Raffle Math

The jar looks full of chances. The arithmetic says how many are yours.

Boundary: arithmetic only Raffle Math turns the counts and dollar amounts you type into odds and averages. It never says whether you should buy tickets, and it does not predict any single draw.

The odds truth

5 of 200 tickets with 3 prizes: a 7.4% chance of at least one win, 92.6% of none. Each ticket can win only one prize.

The value truth

You enter the ticket price and what a prize is worth. 5 tickets at $2.00 cost $10.00, and the average prize value is $3.75. That average comes from your numbers, not from any promise.

The target truth

Name a chance you want. With 3 prizes among 200 tickets, 42 tickets reach 50%.

Run your numbers
Raffle Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
